SLICING 
Get a sublist using slicing

nums[1:4]  #[1,2,3]
nums[:3]  #[0,1,2,3]
nums[:4:] #[4,5,6]
nums[::2]  # [0,2,4,6]
nums [::-1]  #[6,5,4,3,2,1]
