#  SignalForge AI - Trading Signal Analyzer
AI - powered **BUY/SELL signal generator** for
XAU/USD and Crypto pairs.Built with claude API,
Python FastAPI,and Next.js.

---

##  Features

**Chart Screenshot Analysis**- Upload
TradingView/MT4 chart screenshots and get instant 
AI-driven signals.
***Manual Indicator Input**- Support for RSI,
MACD,EMA,and Support/Resistance levels,
***Multi-pair Support** - XAU/USD,BTC/USDT,
ETH/USDT,BNB/USDT,and SOL/USDT.
***Complete Signal Output**- Includes Entry
price,Stoploss (SL),Take Profit(TP),and 
Risk:Reward ratio.
***Confidence Score**-AI-generated
 confidence percentage for every signal.
 ***Telegram Ready**-One-click copy formatted
 signals,ready for Telegram groups.
 ***Signal History**- Easily track your last 5 
 signals.

 ---
